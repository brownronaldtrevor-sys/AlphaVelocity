ALPHA VELOCITY RESEARCH-ONLY RUN

1. Keep Trader Workstation open and logged into Simulated Trading.
2. Keep config.yaml set to mode: PAPER and dry_run: true.
3. Double-click RUN_RESEARCH_ONLY.bat.
4. Wait. IBKR requests are intentionally serialized; five symbols may take time.
5. The command prints a report and saves JSON under research_reports.
6. No strategy order is submitted. This launcher never calls place_bracket.

The included technical scores are hypotheses, not proven edge. They are for observing
market-data flow and report behavior before model training or paper orders.
