# Investment Philosophy - Alpha Velocity Equity Research

**Date**: 2026-07-16  
**Framework**: Opportunity Marketplace v1  
**Scope**: Active Equity Research & Paper Trading  

---

## CORE THESIS

Alpha Velocity seeks overlooked micro-cap and small-cap companies with asymmetric common-equity repricing potential.

We believe value discovery is the necessary but not sufficient precondition for outperformance. Identifying an opportunity requires:

1. **Plausible repricing magnitude** (3x, 5x, or 10x equity scenarios)
2. **Clear survivability** (equity remains intact until catalyst)
3. **Timely recognition** (technical setup confirms market acceptance)
4. **Progressive capital deployment** (entry on breakouts, scaling with volume confirmation)

---

## WHAT WE VALUE

### Economic Catalysts

- Normalized earnings or free-cash-flow recovery
- Operating leverage inflection
- Asset or sum-of-parts value realization
- Capital-stack improvement (refinancing, debt reduction)
- Ownership change or activist involvement
- Strategic review or business restructuring
- Management change or insider alignment
- Margin inflection
- Industry recovery or end-market demand shift

### Market Inefficiencies

- Valuation disconnects (trading below normalized earnings power)
- Liquidity disconnects (small-cap neglect creating mispricing)
- Thesis neglect (market unaware of catalyst or progress)
- Narrative disconnects (changing business not yet reflected in valuation)
- Estimate revisions (upside surprises as reality improves)

### Technical Confirmation

- Completed weekly structure (base formation, support, resistance clear)
- Daily breakout on volume (price acceptance of new level)
- Relative strength (leading sector peers, market)
- Relative volume acceleration (growing participation)
- Constructive pullbacks (buyers defending support)
- Failed breakdowns reversals (failed seller attempts)
- Proximity to invalidation (defined risk, reward/risk attractive)

### Fundamental Confirmation

- Plausible bear, base and bull scenarios
- Bear case survivability (maintains equity value)
- Base case provides positive expected value
- Bull case offers 3x+ upside
- Highest-sensitivity assumptions identified and monitored
- Key catalysts documented and timed

### Capital Structure Confidence

- Adequate liquidity runway (18+ months without cash burn or refinancing)
- Manageable maturities (refinancing risk low)
- Interest coverage sufficient (not distressed)
- Covenant compliance maintained
- Dilution understood and bounded
- Preferred and senior claims quantified
- Restructuring probability assessed

---

## WHAT WE AVOID

### Prohibition Conditions

1. **Cheapness alone** - Low valuation without catalyst or business improvement
2. **Momentum alone** - Technical strength without fundamental reason for move
3. **Win-rate optimization** - Chasing high-probability small-gain trades
4. **Narrative-only trades** - Stories without numeric support or catalysts
5. **Forced full investment** - Holding cash is a valid allocation
6. **Distressed capital structures** - Equity has severe bankruptcy risk
7. **Untradeable liquidity** - Too thin to execute or exit responsibly
8. **Unresolved data quality** - Can't confirm facts or verify sources

### Risk Discipline

1. **Defined invalidation** - Every thesis must have a clear "wrong" condition
2. **Reasonable horizons** - No 10-year research theses for 20-day positions
3. **Position sizing** - Volatility-aware, risk-budget constrained
4. **Correlation awareness** - Avoiding destructive concentration
5. **Downside boundaries** - Expected drawdown does not exceed risk budget
6. **Capital discipline** - Rotation cost and switching friction matter

## DISCOVERY APPROACH

The Opportunity Marketplace runs 8 independent research lenses continuously:

### 1. ASYMMETRIC_EQUITY Lens
Surface candidates where:
- Enterprise value is clear and documentable
- Common equity has asymmetric scenarios (3x, 5x, 10x cases)
- Capital stack is well-defined and survivable
- Normalized earnings or FCF are identifiable

### 2. SURVIVABILITY_AND_CAPITAL_STACK Lens
Assess:
- Cash position and liquidity runway
- Debt seniority and maturities
- Interest coverage and covenant compliance
- Refinancing risk and access to markets
- Dilution and restructuring probability
- Equity value in bear case

### 3. CHART_AND_RECOGNITION Lens
Detect with numeric provenance:
- Completed weekly structure (base, support/resistance clear)
- Daily breakout (price confirms buyer demand)
- Relative strength (peer and market outperformance)
- Relative volume (growing participation)
- Triggers (objective breakout levels)
- Invalidation (defined risk bounds)

### 4. CORPORATE_EVENT_AND_ACTIVIST Lens
Track:
- SEC filings (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K events)
- Insider transactions (Form 4 buying, selling, vesting)
- Activist positions (13D, 13G, proxy contests)
- Strategic review announcements
- Asset sale or restructuring progress
- Refinancing or debt reduction milestones
- Management or board changes

### 5. EXPECTATIONS_AND_REVISIONS Lens
Monitor:
- Revenue, EBITDA, EPS consensus
- Estimate direction (revisions up/down)
- Estimate dispersion (disagreement signals opportunity)
- Company guidance changes
- Normalized earnings bridges
- Alpha Velocity bear/base/bull scenarios
- Expectation gaps (consensus vs. reality)

### 6. MANAGEMENT_LANGUAGE_CHANGE Lens
Compare current with prior filings and calls:
- Material demand changes (market strength/weakness)
- Pricing discussion (improving or deteriorating)
- Backlog statements (forward visibility)
- Margin commentary (cost/pricing dynamics)
- Liquidity or refinancing discussion
- Strategic alternatives exploration
- Capital allocation intentions

### 7. TOP_DOWN_MARKET_AND_INDUSTRY Lens
Rank sectors and industries by:
- Absolute and relative performance
- Performance acceleration
- Estimate revision direction and breadth
- Margin trends and expectations
- Demand indicators and visibility
- Pricing power and competition
- Industry-specific catalysts

Select best companies within improving groups:
- Highest operating leverage within group
- Strongest relative position (market share, costs)
- Clearest catalyst within group
- Highest research confidence in thesis

### 8. TURTLE_TREND_RESEARCH_LENS (Futures-Focused, Zero Production Influence)
Contributes only as independent research opinion:
- Trend channel identification
- Breakout state and channel exit
- ATR/volatility-normalized risk sizing
- Trend maturity assessment
- Trend direction (long or short)

## RESEARCH QUEUES AND ACTIONABILITY

### Trade-Ready Tiers
1. **ACTIONABLE_TRIGGERED** - Breakout triggered, calibrated probability, confirmed structure
2. **STARTER_POSITION_CANDIDATE** - Breakout triggered, research-grade confidence
3. **NEAR_TRIGGER** - Setup complete, awaiting price trigger

### Active Research Tiers
4. **ASYMMETRIC_VALUE_RESEARCH** - 3x+ scenarios identified, survivability confirmed
5. **CHART_MOMENTUM_RESEARCH** - Structure complete, awaiting breakout confirmation
6. **TOP_DOWN_INDUSTRY_RESEARCH** - Group leading, company best-of-breed candidate
7. **EVENT_ACTIVIST_RESEARCH** - Catalyst identified, timing and impact unclear
8. **SPECIAL_SITUATION** - Multiple complex signals requiring case-by-case analysis
9. **HUMAN_HYPOTHESIS** - Researcher-authored thesis with explicit zero allocation influence

### Monitoring & Exclusion Tiers
10. **CURRENT_HOLDING_REVIEW** - Active position requiring thesis refresh
11. **WAITING_FOR_CONFIRMATION** - Setup incomplete or catalyst timing uncertain
12. **HIGH_RISK_SPECULATIVE** - Severe capital-structure or execution risk
13. **EXCLUDED** - Hard exclusions (stale data, untradeable liquidity, etc.)

### Committee Top Five Priority
1. ACTIONABLE_TRIGGERED (ranked by expected value)
2. STARTER_POSITION_CANDIDATE (ranked by expected value)
3. NEAR_TRIGGER (ranked by proximity to trigger)
4. Highest research-confidence candidates (explicitly marked research-only)

If fewer than five actionable candidates exist, fallback to research-only mode with explicit labeling.

## EXPECTED VALUE FRAMEWORK

Every opportunity requires three score components:

### Intrinsic Opportunity Score (Scoring Model Element 1)
- Mispricing magnitude (valuation vs. normalized earnings)
- Capital structure clarity
- Refinancing progress
- Management capability
- Margin recovery visibility
- Asset value realization
- Downside protection

**Scoring**: 0-100, with 60+ considered compelling

### Timing Opportunity Score (Scoring Model Element 2)
- Weekly structure completion
- Daily trigger proximity
- Volatility compression state
- Catalyst timing
- Relative volume confirmation
- Failed-breakdown reversals
- Support/resistance clarity

**Scoring**: 0-100, with 70+ considered near-ready

### Expected Swing Value (Scoring Model Element 3)
- Probability × Expected Move − Costs − Uncertainty
- Caps for severe risk
- Adjustments for liquidity
- Calibration status

**Scoring**: 0-100, calibrated on historical validation

### Combined Rank
```
Final Rank = (0.30 × Intrinsic) + (0.30 × Timing) + (0.40 × Swing Value)
```

This framework prevents:
- Cheapness alone from winning (must have intrinsic score)
- Technical strength alone from winning (must have timing + swing value)
- Uncalibrated models from dominating allocation (swing value caps)

## DISCOVERY + INFLECTION INTELLIGENCE (v1)

Alpha Velocity evaluates opportunities on four independent horizons:
- Research horizon
- Primary repricing horizon
- Tactical swing horizon
- Execution horizon

Inflection is treated as a multi-dimensional process, not a single score. We track whether each dimension is before, within, or after transition and whether dimensions are synchronized or contradictory.

Required interpretive rules:
- Synchronization strength can improve confidence but does not bypass risk or governance.
- Recognition gap can indicate timing opportunity but does not override invalidation discipline.
- Momentum and pattern quality are evidence inputs, not stand-alone allocation authority.
- Future outlook statements must separate facts, guidance, estimates, and inference.
- Unknowns must be explicit rather than silently assumed favorable.

Ranking integration boundary:
- Discovery intelligence may be passed as shadow signals.
- Shadow influence remains disabled by default and must be explicitly enabled.

## CAPITAL ALLOCATION MODEL

### Cash Competes
Cash is an explicit competing allocation. When no opportunity exceeds hurdle rates or risk constraints:
- Maintain cash position
- Reduce position weights
- Rotate slowly into quality

### Position Sizing
Sizes vary by:
- Expected swing value and probability
- Liquidity tier and execution risk
- Volatility and drawdown budget
- Correlation with existing holdings
- Concentration and sector limits

### Rebalancing Discipline
- Rotation cost and spread friction matter
- Switching must justify by expected opportunity cost
- Partial fills and slippage reduce net benefit
- Monthly or quarterly rebalancing, not daily chasing

## RESEARCH CONFIDENCE VS. OPPORTUNITY ATTRACTIVENESS

**Separate metrics:**

- **Research Confidence**: How certain are we the opportunity was correctly identified by lenses? (0-100)
  - Increased by multiple confirming lenses
  - Calibrated opportunity evidence
  - Reduced by model disagreement or conflicting signals

- **Opportunity Attractiveness**: What is the expected value ranking score? (0-100)
  - Intrinsic + Timing + Swing Value model output
  - Independent of research confidence
  - Can be high confidence + low attractiveness (thesis doesn't clear hurdle)
  - Can be high attractiveness + low confidence (strong numeric case but unconfirmed evidence)

Research confidence alone does NOT increase allocation. Attractiveness score does.

## LEARNING AND VALIDATION

Every opportunity must be journaled and graded:
- Forward returns intra-trade, multi-day, multi-week
- Hit rate and payoff ratio
- Calibration accuracy (expected vs. realized probability)
- Opportunity-cost performance
- Thesis accuracy (did stated case play out?)
- False breakout rate
- Catalyst success rate

No evidence stream may promote itself. Promotion remains a separate, governed process.

---

## KNOWN LIMITATIONS

This framework works best when:
- Micro-cap and small-cap research data is available
- SEC filings and transcripts are timely and accessible
- Technical patterns remain stable across regimes
- Management is rational and communicative
- Catalyst timing is somewhat predictable

This framework has poor visibility when:
- Distressed restructuring (equity may be worthless)
- Rapid macro deterioration (valuations reset sharply)
- Complete business transformation (historical patterns break)
- Fraud or undisclosed conflicts (trust betrayed)
- Extreme macro regimes (correlations approach 1.0, diversification fails)

## ETHICAL FRAMEWORK

Alpha Velocity operates within strict governance bounds:
- No insider information
- All research publicly available or freely disclosed
- No market manipulation or layering
- Conflict of interest disclosure
- Position transparency when required
- Market-making liquidity provisioning where applicable

Paper trading only until validation on real money. No recommendation for external capital.
