# Price Action Validation Standard

Price action is an evidence family, not an entitlement to portfolio weight.

## Included features

- close location within the final bar;
- 20-day and 60-day range position;
- higher-high/higher-low trend structure;
- moving-average alignment;
- volatility compression and expansion;
- gaps;
- breakout and breakdown distance;
- failed breakouts and failed breakdowns;
- distance to structural support and resistance;
- relative volume;
- accumulation and distribution proxies; and
- a conservative invalidation level.

## Promotion rule

The live research report always computes these features, but their blending weight defaults to
zero. A feature set earns a maximum 20% evidence weight only after a temporally isolated,
out-of-sample ablation test demonstrates all of the following:

1. at least 500 independent or effectively independent observations;
2. improved Brier score versus the same model without price action;
3. improved log loss;
4. improved net return after modeled costs;
5. stability across at least 60% of defined regimes; and
6. a frozen validation artifact committed or generated by the validation pipeline.

If the validation artifact is missing, stale, rejected, or malformed, price action contributes
zero weight. Failed features remain visible in reports for research but cannot influence capital.

## Bias controls

- finalized bars only;
- no same-close execution assumption;
- labels must be target-before-stop and time bounded;
- purging and embargo for overlapping holding periods;
- multiple-testing counts recorded;
- comparison against momentum, volatility, liquidity, sector, and regime baselines;
- all results reported after costs and failed fills.
