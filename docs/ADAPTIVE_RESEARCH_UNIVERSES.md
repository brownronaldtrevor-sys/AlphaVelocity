# Adaptive Research Universes v1

Adaptive Research Universes broaden discovery across market segments and opportunity pools while preserving one canonical Opportunity model and one downstream pipeline.

## Design Principles
- No single market-cap segment is privileged.
- Discovery is independent by universe, then merged by canonical security identity.
- Universe priority is research guidance, not capital authorization.
- Ranking, committee, risk, governance, and human approval boundaries remain unchanged.

## Supported Universe Types
- MICRO_CAP
- SMALL_CAP
- MID_CAP
- LARGE_CAP
- BROAD_MARKET
- SECTOR
- INDUSTRY
- SPECIAL_SITUATION
- ACTIVIST
- TURNAROUND
- HIGH_VOLATILITY
- DEEP_VALUE
- MOMENTUM
- EVENT_DRIVEN
- CURRENT_HOLDINGS
- USER_WATCHLIST
- ETF_BENCHMARK
- CUSTOM

## Key Outputs
- Point-in-time universe membership records
- Per-universe diagnostics
- Explainable research-priority recommendations
- Merged canonical opportunities with universe provenance
