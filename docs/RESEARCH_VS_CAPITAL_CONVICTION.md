# Research vs Capital Conviction

These are intentionally separate fields on the canonical Opportunity.

## Research Conviction
Question: Is this thesis worth continued underwriting and attention?

Preserves:
- state
- confidence
- evidence
- contradictions
- applicable_horizon
- required_confirmation
- validation_status

## Capital Conviction
Question: Is this currently one of the best uses of capital?

Preserves:
- state
- confidence
- evidence
- contradictions
- applicable_horizon
- required_confirmation
- validation_status

## Key Principle
High research conviction may coexist with low capital conviction.
This separation prevents research quality from becoming implicit execution authorization.
