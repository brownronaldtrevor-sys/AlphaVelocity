# Momentum And Pattern Intelligence v1

## Momentum Profile
The classifier emits a structured momentum profile with:
- Long, medium, and short trend states
- Direction, velocity, persistence, and maturity
- Relative strength versus market, sector, and peers
- Relative volume and liquidity-adjusted momentum
- Volatility state plus acceleration/deceleration

## Pattern Profile
Pattern intelligence includes:
- Pattern name and calculation window
- Trigger/pivot, support, resistance, depth, duration
- Volume and volatility behavior
- Distance to trigger and extension
- Invalidation and quality warnings
- Numeric provenance values

## Usage
Momentum and pattern profiles are explainability artifacts for discovery and committee review. They do not independently authorize execution.
