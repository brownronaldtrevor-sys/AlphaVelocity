# Universe Diagnostics

Per-universe diagnostics are transparent scan outputs and do not alter ranking weights.

## Metrics
- securities_considered
- securities_usable
- candidates_discovered
- candidates_qualified
- duplicate_candidates_merged
- excluded_count
- data_unavailable_count
- actionable_count
- starter_count
- near_trigger_count
- research_only_count
- average_research_confidence
- average_capital_conviction
- early_inflection_count
- primary_move_count
- warning_count

## Research Priority Output
Each universe also provides:
- priority: VERY_HIGH, HIGH, NORMAL, LOW, PAUSED
- reasons
- contradictions
- missing_information
- required_confirmation
- confidence
- validation_status

Universe priority is research guidance only and never execution authorization.
