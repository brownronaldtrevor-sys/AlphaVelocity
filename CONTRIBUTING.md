# Contributing

All changes should:

1. be made on a branch;
2. include tests;
3. preserve paper-only defaults;
4. preserve ethical and governance controls;
5. avoid look-ahead bias;
6. document new parameters and assumptions;
7. pass `python -m pytest -q`; and
8. avoid adding secrets, account data, reports, or local environments.

Model and strategy changes must be treated as research hypotheses until validated
with point-in-time data and walk-forward testing.
